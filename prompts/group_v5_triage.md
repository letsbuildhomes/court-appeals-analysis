## Step 1: document membership and municipal housing scope

Read the documents together. Identify the proceeding they belong to, the
municipal decision challenged, and the housing proposal at issue. A later motion,
reopening, or Supreme Court review retains the identity of the underlying appeal.
A separate historical case does not confer its scope on the current proceeding.

increase, and care facilities with apartments (not beds alone).
